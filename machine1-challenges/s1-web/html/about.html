About NovaTech Corp

Founded in 2018, NovaTech Corp has grown from a small startup into a trusted software solutions provider serving clients across South Asia and beyond.

Our Mission

We believe technology should be accessible, reliable, and secure. Our mission is to deliver enterprise-grade software solutions that scale with your business while maintaining the highest standards of security and performance.

With offices in Colombo and remote teams across the region, we operate a 24/7 development and support model that ensures your systems are always running at peak performance.

Our History

NovaTech Corp was founded in Colombo, Sri Lanka in 2018 by a team of experienced software engineers with backgrounds in fintech and enterprise systems. Within two years, we had expanded our client base to over 50 organisations across banking, healthcare, and logistics sectors.

Today, NovaTech Corp operates as a fully distributed company with more than 120 employees and a portfolio of over 200 successful project deliveries.
